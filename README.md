# Dungeon Crawler

A retro first-person grid dungeon crawler in the browser, in the style of Eye of the Beholder.

Play it at [https://garylarock.github.io/dungeon-crawler/](https://garylarock.github.io/dungeon-crawler/).

## How to play

**Desktop**

- **W / S** — step forward and back
- **A / D** — strafe
- **Q / E** or the arrow keys — turn
- **Space** — open doors
- **R** — restart

**Mobile**

- Tap the on-screen buttons
- Swipe left or right on the view to turn
- Swipe up or down on the view to step
- Tap the view to open doors
